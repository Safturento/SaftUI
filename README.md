# SaftUI

A modular World of Warcraft UI replacement: unit frames and nameplates, action bars, bags and bank, chat, minimap and micro menu, loot rolls, loot feed and loot window, an objective tracker of its own, and a set of flat, pixel-perfect skins for Blizzard frames. Every part is its own addon on top of `SaftUI-Core`. It runs on WoW Forever (interface 16001) and retail (interface 120000).

## Installation

1. Clone the repo.
2. Put each `SaftUI-*` folder in your game's `Interface/AddOns` folder for the client you play (e.g. `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns`). For development, link them instead of copying. On Windows, use directory symlinks so the game sees folders:

   ```bat
   mklink /D "E:\Games\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\SaftUI-Core" "C:\path\to\SaftUI-Modular\SaftUI-Core"
   ```

   Repeat for each `SaftUI-*` folder. From WSL, run it through `cmd.exe /c`. A WSL `ln -s` link shows up as a file to the game, not a folder.
3. Enable the SaftUI addons on the character select screen. `SaftUI-Core` is required by all the others.

SaftUI sizes itself to your screen: it is laid out for a screen 1440 pixels high and drawn at the same share of yours, with every line on whole pixels. It sets Blizzard's UI Scale setting back after it moves. Global settings > General > UI scale has a UI size over the automatic size, Pixel for pixel (every size that many pixels, as before), and The game's UI scale, which leaves the size to Blizzard's setting.

## Usage

- `/sui` (or `/saftui`) opens the options window. The sidebar on the left has the search box at the top, with Edit mode and Keybind mode buttons under it, then Global Settings, Profiles and every module's page, grouped under Frames, Combat and Info. A page's settings sit in titled sections, two columns of a label and its control, with small ones (checkboxes) two to a column, each section on its own darker panel with faint lines between the columns and between the rows, and lines of buttons off the panels, on the window's own background (Copy from's Confirm copy and Cancel sit as far under its settings as above the dialog's bottom; a button right under the note that explains it, as Profiles' Reset Profile, stays on the panel with it; the Blizzard look keeps the plain layout). A setting changed from its default shows a small reset arrow just left of its control: hover it for the default, click it to put that back. A position is one block: click a dot on each square for which point of the frame goes to which point of what it's attached to, then type X and Y or nudge them with the arrows (Shift for 10). Tabs along the top switch between a page's parts, and on pages with many of one thing (unit frames, action bars) a dropdown picks which one. Hover a setting's name for what it does. The search box finds settings by name across every page; click a result to go to it. Drag the window by its title to move it (it remembers where), drag its bottom edge, or the grip at its bottom right corner, to make it taller (it remembers its height too), and close it with the X at the top right, Close at the bottom, or Escape. The arrow beside the X shrinks the window to half its width, the page in one column, and the arrow that then appears grows it back (under the Blizzard look they are Blizzard's own red condense and expand buttons, the same as its other windows' maximize and minimize buttons); the window's right edge and top stay where they were, `/sui` opens at the size you left it, and a screen too narrow for the full window opens it at half. At half the same arrow shrinks it again to a small bar at the window's top right corner, for seeing the whole UI: "SaftUI", the page's name and the tab's, an arrow to grow it back and the X. Drag the bar anywhere to move it, or click its names (or the arrow) to grow it back to half, on the same page at the same scroll; `/sui`, a click on a frame in edit mode and Edit this category in a bag grow it too. Esc, the X and entering combat close it (it opens again at half). The button at the right of the sidebar's foot collapses the sidebar: the window narrows from its left, the page and the right edge stay where they are, and the same button, now at the footer's left, shows it again (at half too; `/sui` remembers it). While it is collapsed Search, Edit mode and Keybind mode are icon buttons in the footer beside that button (Search opens its box above it), and the page's title gets a small arrow that opens the list of pages. Reload UI is at the bottom right, beside Close. On a unit frame's page, the Unit dropdown's line holds Copy from (it opens a small window that copies the settings you tick from another unit, each named as its tab or setting is elsewhere in `/sui`), then the unit frame profile every unit frame uses (a dropdown named Profile) and Manage, which opens the window that makes, copies, renames, deletes, exports and imports them; the nameplate pages have the profile and Manage without Copy from, and at half the line wraps to two.
- `/sui` → Global Settings opens on General: UI scale first (Scale: Automatic, Pixel for pixel or The game's UI scale; UI size, which applies when you let go of the slider and then asks you to keep it; and a line saying what this screen gets), then Icon zoom (how much of each edge SaftUI cuts off the icons it skins), with a preview icon under it, Title bar height (the strips along the top and bottom of the bag and bank windows; under the Blizzard theme a window in Blizzard's metal holds its title in the metal's own title bar instead) and Screen edge margin (how far in from the screen's edges all of SaftUI's frames are kept: bars, unit frames, bags, chat, the options window and everything edit mode moves; the minimap, which is Blizzard's, only when you drag it; below 0 they can go past the edges. Chat, the swing timers and the default tooltip also go no nearer the edges than Blizzard keeps them: chat stays at least 32 in from the left and bottom, 25 from the right and 60 from the top, and the others stay on screen). Title bar height and the margin show at once. Icon zoom's preview changes as you drag the slider, and the icons across SaftUI after a reload (Reload UI, bottom right).
- `/sui` → Global Settings → Fonts sets each of SaftUI's fonts: its face, size, outline, shadow offset and line spacing, which every text in that font shows at once. Each font has its job as a note, and a reset icon by each setting that differs from its default. Kinds of text, last, picks the font for button text (the label of every SaftUI button, such as the bags' Bag Slots and Warband Bank buttons and keybind mode's Done, the totem bar's key bindings, counts and names, and the bag slot bars), which changes at once. Heading is also the font of the bags' category titles and of SaftUI's tracker's header line and zone headers, and its face draws `/sui`'s own titles. Every Font row in `/sui` lists SaftUI's fonts first and then every typeface, each in its own face, with a link beside a shared font that opens its section of this tab, or, beside a typeface, three dots that open its own menu: Size, Outline, Shadow offset X and Y and Line spacing for that one text (some rows show fewer).
- `/sui` → Global Settings → Colors edits SaftUI's colors, in sections Text, Buttons, Class, Roles, Reputation, Factions, Power, Item quality, Loot, Status, Threat and Objectives. Each color is a field with the color's swatch at its left (a see-through one over a checker) and its code beside it; click it to open SaftUI's color picker (every color field in `/sui` opens it): a square for shade, strips for hue and, where the color has one, opacity, boxes for red, green, blue, opacity and a hex code, your last 12 colors, and, for every color on the page and the Accent, a preview of the color in use under the square (text, buttons, bars, nameplates, slots and loot feed entries, as the color is drawn). Changes show as you make them; Okay keeps the color, and Cancel or Esc puts the old one back. Hover a color for where SaftUI shows it; a color you've changed has a reset arrow by its field that puts SaftUI's default back. Buttons holds one color, Danger (locked bank slots, and the flash on an action bar or totem button), with an opacity that sets how strongly it is drawn (100% is SaftUI's own). A profile that changed Buttons > Normal, Hover, Pushed or Checked, which the states no longer use, loses those colors once, at the update, and nothing else. Every color shows everywhere as you pick it, with no reload: this window takes it when the picker closes, a tooltip the next time it shows, and chat lines already printed keep theirs. After a profile switch the zone faction words (on the zone text and the world map) take the new profile's colors the next time they're drawn, at the next zone you enter or hover.
- `/sui` → Global Settings → Theme sets how SaftUI draws its frames: Thick (a dark line on each side of the border), Thin (just the border) or Blizzard (Blizzard's own art: the character frame's metal around windows, with the window's title and its buttons in the metal's title bar, the bag slot bars above it, and Blizzard's red close button in its corner, the tooltip border on panels, Blizzard's button art on action bars and bag slots, and Blizzard's own look for buttons, check boxes, tabs, scroll bars and `/sui`'s controls; `/sui` itself looks like Blizzard's Settings window, in the metal with its inner frame, banners over the sidebar's groups and Settings dropdowns, sliders and scroll bars, unit frames wear Blizzard's unit frame box, party and raid frames sit in Blizzard's raid group border and all of them use Blizzard's bar fills, and the micro menu bar stands on Blizzard's micro menu and minimap art, Blizzard's grey minimap buttons on retail), the chosen theme's Border, Outer border (Thick only), Window background (Blizzard only, the fill of windows) and Background colors (under Blizzard, the Background is the fill of everything but windows; both are Blizzard's dark grey to start with; the Background's picker has an opacity, 100% for a solid background), each with a reset arrow by its field while it differs from the chosen theme's default (the Background's puts its opacity back too). Accent is SaftUI's one accent color, blue to start with, for every theme: what is on, picked, current or under the mouse in SaftUI's frames and windows (`/sui`'s sidebar, tabs, sliders and check boxes, edit mode, a selected mover, links and your own marks on shared settings), the bags' Category: tooltip line, the rule editor's AND, the bags' loading bar, the loot roll timer bars and your choice on a held roll, an equipped item's border on the action bars, SaftUI's tracker, and the prefix of SaftUI's chat messages. Every state of every button, field, row and slot in SaftUI is made from it by rule: under the mouse (hover) is a tint of it over the widget, pressed a stronger one, picked or current has an Accent edge and a fade, typing in a field lights its edge, and disabled dims. The dots at the right of the Accent row open a menu that sets how: Derivation (Layers, Tones or Hue-shifted tones), the Strengths of Hover, Pushed, Selected fill, Selected fade and Disabled, the Tones' Lightness step and the Hue shift's Hue shift, Direction and Chroma change, with a strip that shows Rest, Hover, Pushed, Selected, Selected and hovered and Disabled as you move a setting, and a reset arrow by each setting you changed. Text on a tinted widget keeps its color. It has no opacity. Everything that draws in it follows it as you pick it. A profile that changed Buttons > Blue keeps that color as its accent. Options windows sets the Background of SaftUI's own windows (`/sui` and its small dialogs, edit mode's bar, its floating window and its History list): Use SaftUI's theme, the default, makes them see-through like everything else while the Background is; Solid never is. Lists, menus, the search panel and the color picker, which open over other text, stay solid. Window titles sets the font of the titles of SaftUI's windows (the bags and bank, `/sui`'s title bar under Blizzard, and Blizzard's color picker's title while Skinning dresses it), Text to start with, for every theme. Each theme keeps its own colors, so switching back brings yours back. Every change shows at once.
- Every module's page has its own Theme and Background (on a unit frame, each part's menu or tab; the bags also have a Slot theme and Slot background for item and currency slots). Use SaftUI's theme, the default, follows Global Settings → Theme; pick Thick, Thin, Blizzard or None (no border or background), and Solid or Transparent (at the Background's opacity), to override it there. Profiles saved before themes look as they did: their old choices become a Theme and Background by themselves, on login, on switching to them, and on importing an old unit frame profile.
- `/sui scale` says the UI size SaftUI draws at and what it makes of your screen. `/sui scale 90` (or `90%`, or `1.25` for 125%) tries Automatic at that size, from 70 to 150, and `/sui scale auto`, `pixel` or `game` tries that mode; each asks "Keep this UI size?" and goes back by itself after 15 seconds, or when you press Esc or Go back. `/sui scale reset` puts back Automatic at 100% at once, with no question: use it if SaftUI is ever too large or too small to use.
- `/sui debug` turns SaftUI's debug prints on or off (off to start with; it remembers across reloads and characters). They're for tracking down a problem; right now they show each step a held roll bar goes through (your choice, the bar being held, its drop, its finish and its release).
- `/suifonts` opens a temporary demo window for choosing a small-text font: today's two fonts and nine candidates, each shown on the kinds of text SaftUI uses it for, with a size slider, outline choices, a shadow toggle and a scale. Click a name to star a font. It is only for trying fonts in the game (the game must be fully restarted once to see the new font files) and goes away when a font is chosen. The candidates are also in `/sui` → Global Settings → Fonts, named "Candidate: ...".
- `/sui edit` toggles SaftUI's edit mode. Drag frames, nudge them with the arrow keys (Shift for 10px), and click a frame to open its settings. Where frames overlap, a list by the cursor shows which one you'd grab, and Alt switches to the next; only the one under the mouse shows its name. It also turns on when Blizzard's Edit Mode opens. A small two-row bar at the top edge of the screen (drag its blank parts to slide it along the edge; a button hides it until the mouse reaches the edge), shown with `/sui` open or closed, holds its controls: Exit, Grid (an alignment grid drawn out from the screen's center lines) with its Grid size (type a number, or use the arrows, 4 at a time), Snap to grid and Snap to frames, which pull a dragged frame onto grid lines or other frames' edges, Dim world (darkens the game world behind your frames, on to start with) and Coordinates (shows the selected frame's position by the corner it's anchored by, like "Top left of the screen: 120, -330", following a drag), Show disabled and Show hidden (movers of frames turned off, or not on screen now like a closed bag or no pet; hidden ones show to start with), a Show list to pick which addons' frames show, a filter box (type a name, Enter selects the first match), Undo and Redo (a drag, a run of arrow-key nudges, a Reset and a unit frames' Copy from are one step each, and a tooltip says what was undone), History (lists your changes, newest first: click one to go back to it, or revert just that one; it keeps 50 changes to start with, up to 1000, set in the list or under Global Settings → Edit mode), Reset (puts the selected frame back in its default place; Undo takes it back) and Options, which opens the selected frame's page, or Global Settings → Edit mode with none. Detach, at the bar's end, turns the bar into a small window with the same controls under Grid and snap, Show and Changes: drag it by its title (it can't leave the screen margin, it sits over `/sui`, and the History list opens beside it) and click Dock in its title to put the bar back; which of the two you left, and where the window sits, are remembered across reloads. The bar, or the window, fades while you drag a frame under it. The same settings are under `/sui` → Global Settings → Edit mode. The history empties when you switch profile or the unit frame profile. Hold Ctrl while dragging to move freely. Dragging and nudging stop at the screen edge margin (Global Settings → General). Edit mode and the options window close when combat starts.
- `/sui` → Unitframes → Party, Raid 10, Raid 40 or Boss → General → Test mode fills that spot with made-up group members, or for Boss made-up bosses, styled and laid out like the real frames, so the Layout settings can be checked without a group or out of an encounter. The made-up frames make way while real ones show, and Test mode is off after a reload.
- A unit frame's page has five tabs: General, Health, Power (on Player with Power bar and Additional power under it), Cast bar and Auras (Buffs and Debuffs under it). General's sections are Frame, Position, and Parts: the name, the health and power texts, the icons and Class power as two small rows to a line, each with a check box, a word for what its position is attached to, and a dots menu of its other settings (click the row or the dots). The health and power texts' settings are also on the Health and Power tabs; a change in one shows in the other.
- `/sui` → Unit frames opens on All units: the look, parts, bars and auras every unit follows until it has its own (Theme and Background, each part and tab, but not a unit's Enable, size, place, layout or Class power and Additional power, nor the nameplates). Change a setting on a unit and it is that unit's own, marked on its row; the row's Reset (hover it) makes it follow All units again, and Apply to hands a value to All or to the units you pick. On All units a setting some unit owns has a small diff icon that lists who. The first time SaftUI starts with a unit frame profile it sets All units up from what each unit shows, so nothing changes on screen, and keeps a backup. `/sui uf restore` (then `/sui uf restore confirm`) puts the units' shared settings back as they were before, with no reload, and no login turns sharing on again until you type `/sui uf share` and then `/sui uf share confirm`.
- SaftUI draws the nameplates too, on by default, set on two pages after Raid 40: `/sui` → Unitframes → Enemy nameplates (enemies and neutral units) and Friendly nameplates. An enemy's plate is a 200 by 20 health bar with its name inside on the left, cut short before the health, and its health inside on the right in short form (12.5K); its castbar hangs underneath with the spell's icon at the left, grey when the cast can't be interrupted; up to 6 of your debuffs (your pet's too) sit above it, up to 4 of its buffs you can purge to its right, a quest icon to its left on units your quests are about, and its raid mark above. Your target's plate has a white border under Thick and Thin, and Blizzard's target art under Blizzard (a friend's plate that's only a name has neither). In a party or raid an enemy's bar turns orange when your threat on it is a warning and red when it's bad, by your role: as damage or healing, about to pull it or pulling it; as a tank, about to lose it, not holding it (unless another tank in your group does), or loose on someone who isn't a tank. Your threat on it shows as a percent above the bar's right end, in the same color, or in the normal text color while it's fine (Global Settings → Colors → Threat has Warning and Bad). Enemy nameplates' General has Nameplates (it asks to reload), Threat when solo (the tint and the percent when you're on your own too), Use friendly nameplates' settings (below), the plate's Theme and Background, and Width and Height: enemy plates' size and the area you click, and the size the game spaces every plate by; its Health, Cast bar and Auras tabs and the Parts on General (name, subtext, health text, raid mark, quest icon and the threat percent) set each part as on any unit frame. There's no Position or Copy from on either page: the game places each plate. The plates keep whole-pixel borders as they move. While they're on, SaftUI sets the game's nameplate settings they need (friendly plates by Friendly plates, every plate at one scale, no simplified plates) and puts your own back when you turn Nameplates off. With Plater, EllesmereUI Nameplates, KuiNameplates, Threat Plates, NeatPlates, Platynator, or ElvUI with its own nameplates on, SaftUI leaves the nameplates to that addon, and both pages say which addon draws them.
- A friendly player's or NPC's plate is just its name to start with: centred where the plate is, a player's in their class color and an NPC's in the friendly reaction color (green), with its raid mark above and no bar. Click the name to target the unit: what you click is the name's box, not a whole plate around it. Friendly nameplates' General has Friendly plates (SaftUI's plates over friendly players and NPCs outside instances and Blizzard's inside them; off, none), Use enemy nameplates' settings (below), the plate's Theme and Background, and Width and Height (150 by 16 to start, centred on the plate: what's drawn and what you click). Its other tabs set each part, so a friend's plate can have a health bar, a castbar, debuffs, buffs or a quest icon too; it never shows threat. A plate that changes hands between an enemy and a friend, or a unit that changes sides (a duel), takes the other look at once, in combat too.
- Each nameplate page has a box to use the other page's settings. Use enemy nameplates' settings (on Friendly nameplates) draws friendly plates exactly as enemy plates, but never with threat; Use friendly nameplates' settings (on Enemy nameplates) draws enemy plates with Friendly nameplates' settings, all but threat, which stays on Enemy nameplates and still shows, and every plate then takes Friendly nameplates' Width and Height as its size. While a box is checked, its page's other tabs stay where they are but are faded out and can't be clicked (Threat stays usable on Enemy nameplates), and General keeps only the on/off switches and the box, with "Sourced from enemy nameplates" (or friendly) centred where the rest was, so nothing there seems to do nothing; your settings are kept and come back when you uncheck it. Only one box can be on: while one is, the other page's is greyed out, and hovering it says why.
- Both nameplate pages have a Subtext tab, after Name: a second line under a plate's name, an NPC's title (`<Innkeeper>`) or a player's guild (`<Some Guild>`), in angle brackets and light grey. Its NPC titles and Player guilds boxes turn each on; both are on for friendly plates and off for enemy plates to start with. A unit with neither gets no second line and its name stays where it is, and a pet's or guardian's owner line isn't shown. The tab also sets the line's font, color, opacity, max width and position: under the name on a friendly plate, under the castbar's row on an enemy's. You can't click the line, and where the game hides a unit's info it's hidden.
- On every unit frame's Auras tab, under Buffs, Buttons → Only buffs I can purge shows only the buffs you can take off the unit with a purge, dispel or steal spell you know (none if you know none), and follows your spells as you learn them or summon your felhunter; it's on for the nameplates. It and Only mine apply without a reload, and so do Size, Relative size and Max (a plate that changes between an enemy and a friend in combat gets its new button size once combat ends); the borders and Cooldown → Show still need one. On nameplates a plate's buttons take those two from the first page that shows that area on it, which is Enemy nameplates whenever it has that area on, so Friendly nameplates' reach friendly plates only while Enemy nameplates has it off (their tooltips say so). Health text → Short (a part on General, also on the Health tab's Text section) shows the health as 12.5K or 1.2M, the Name part's Max width cuts the name at that many pixels, and its Color friendly colors friendly units' names in the friendly reaction color (on for friendly nameplates, off elsewhere; units that show only players, such as Player, Party and the raids, don't have it).
- `/sui` → Action Bars opens on All bars: the settings every bar follows, its buttons, buttons per row, size, spacing, which corner its first button sits in (First button: the buttons fill across from that corner, the next row away from it, and the bar itself doesn't move), visibility (always, in combat only, or faded until moused over) and its four texts, with the Theme and Background every button is drawn in. Pick a bar (1–8, the pet bar, the stance bar) to turn it on or off, place it, or give it a setting of its own: a changed setting is the bar's own (a blue bar at its left), and hovering its name offers a reset arrow (follow All bars again) and Apply to: All or Multiple (pick bars). On All bars an icon by a setting, in Muted (Global Settings → Colors → Text → Muted, the same grey as hints), lists the bars that keep their own, with their values. The texts (Hotkey, Stack count, Macro name, Cooldown) sit on one line, each with a Show box and three dots that open its font, size, outline and place (the font is one of SaftUI's fonts, Small text to start with, which brings its own size and outline and follows its changes on the Fonts tab, or any typeface, which takes the text's Size and Outline); the pet and stance bars have the hotkey and the cooldown numbers only. All bars' Cooldown box is Blizzard's own Show Numbers for Cooldowns, so turning it on or off in either place does both. A setting changed from its default gets a small reset arrow by its control. Every bar is an edit mode frame, and clicking one opens its page. The pet bar shows while you have a pet out (right-click a pet ability to toggle its autocast), and the stance bar shows one button per stance or form your class has. The extra action button, the zone ability and a leave vehicle button are edit mode frames too, with their own size. The leave vehicle button shows in a vehicle you can leave; on a flight path, where it asks for an early landing; and while you control a creature (mind control or a quest), where it ends the possess.
- `/sui kb` (or "Keybind mode" in `/sui`, the keyboard icon in the footer while the sidebar is collapsed) binds keys to SaftUI's action bars by hovering. Hover a button and press a key or mouse button, with any modifiers; right-click a button to clear its keys; Escape leaves. Bars set to show only in combat or faded show while it's on; to bind pet bar keys this way, have a pet out. SaftUI's bars use Blizzard's own action bar bindings, so binds show in Blizzard's Key Bindings menu, and the binds you already have keep working.
- Bag, bank, warband bank and combined bank windows have four views. Compact, the default, keeps the categories in columns. One bag puts every bag's items in one grid, with special bags (the reagent bag, or on Forever a quiver or a profession bag, in the bank's bag slots too) and Currencies under it. Multi bag gives each bag or bank tab its own titled section. A special bag's title says what kind it is after its name, in grey (`Shipwright's Satchel · Reagent Bag (6 / 6)`). Rows gives each category a full-width row. Four buttons in the window's title bar, left of the close button (One bag, Multi bag, Compact, Rows), switch between them, and the current view's button is lit in the accent (`/sui` → Global Settings → Theme → Accent). Each window remembers its own view, so the bank can be Rows while your bags stay Compact; the same choice is `/sui` → Bags → (the window) → General → View. A switch made in combat applies when combat ends. A one-column window widens just enough that its title clears the buttons, and a window that scrolls is wide enough for its scroll bar to sit inside it.
- `/sui` → Bags → (the window) → General → Empty slots (Off, Grouped, Every slot) shows free slots as an Empty Slots category, in every bag and bank window, Off to start with. Grouped is one slot for each kind of bag (your bags, the reagent bag, the bank, the warband bank) and for each bag with its own section in One bag and Multi bag, each with its free count; drop an item on it to put the item in the first free slot. Items in the reagent bag carry a green wedge in the top right of the icon. Every item auto vendor will sell carries Blizzard's junk coin in the top left of its icon, whether or not you are at a merchant, and the footer's gold tooltip shows the Vendor profit of them all, in every view. Auto vendor sells the items in the Grays/Auto Vendor category (by default grays, legacy gear, and items you marked Auto Vendor in the slot menu with Ctrl + right click, which pins them there; the same menu has Edit this category and Add to category..., and an item's tooltip names its category and the block that caught it), wherever that category stands in the list and whatever it is called, and nothing if you turn it off. It never sells an item with no vendor price, a Protected item, an item in an equipment set, a Cosmetic item, an item whose tooltip says Fishing or Blizzard Account Bound, or one whose tooltip hasn't arrived yet. It sells grays first, then the rest, the cheapest stack first, so what stays in the merchant's buyback list (it keeps only the last 12 you sell) is the most valuable. Grays go at once. If more than 12 stacks that aren't gray would sell at a visit (a rule you edited that catches a lot, say), it sells the grays, then asks, with how many, what they fetch at a vendor and the few most valuable by name: Sell them, which sells just those stacks, or Keep them (or Esc), which sells nothing more at that merchant and asks again at the next one. Enter doesn't answer it. If a fight starts while it is up it goes away and comes back after the fight. With 12 or fewer it doesn't ask, and everything it sold without asking is still in the buyback list, unless you then answer Sell them to a later question; that question says how many stacks sold earlier this visit it would push out. A visit that is waiting on a stack the game has locked or not yet described checks the bags every 2 seconds until the merchant closes. It says in chat what it sold. `/sui` → Bags → General → Merge stacks shows stacks of one item as a single slot, kept apart for the reagent bag and your other bags.
- `/sui` → Bags → Categories lists every bag category in the order they claim items (an item goes to the first one that catches it), with how many of your bag items each holds, `edited` on a default you changed and `yours` on one you made, and a line through any that's turned off. Drag one by its handle to move it (Miscellaneous stays last). Select one to edit it on the right: its name (no two categories can share one), On, Show in (the windows that show it), what it catches as an outline of blocks joined by AND or OR (click AND or OR to flip it; + Block, + Group, NOT, drag a block by its handle, the X removes it) and Delete, each of the first four with a reset icon that puts back what SaftUI ships, and, in a footer that stays at the editor's bottom while the rest scrolls, the items it catches in your bags now (hover one to see the block that caught it; its tooltip opens beside the window, so it covers nothing in it) and the items pinned to it (the X unpins one). The list and the editor scroll on their own with the mouse wheel, and hold a dragged category near the list's top or bottom, or a dragged block near the editor's, to scroll it. When `/sui` is at half width, the list is a menu on a button over the editor, which then has the whole width: the button names the category you are editing and its place (`3 of 31`), the arrows beside it step to the previous or next category, and the menu has the same rows, counts and handles (drag to reorder) and closes when you pick one. + New category adds one, or brings back a default you deleted, and a reset icon after it puts the shipped categories back in SaftUI's order (yours stay right after the one they follow) while they are out of it. A default you bring back while another category has its name gets a number after it (Toys (2)), so names stay apart; a Name reset waits until the name is free. Changes apply to the bags at once. A block not filled in yet (no text, nothing ticked, no number, no Yes or No) or an empty group catches nothing, and an AND holding one catches nothing until it's finished, so a half-done edit never makes a category catch more than before; for Grays/Auto Vendor that means nothing sells until the edit is finished. For Item level and Required level, type the number first, then pick at least, at most or between.
- Hover an item in a bag window and its tooltip ends with two lines: `Category:` (the category it's in, and its place in the list) and `Caught by:` (the block that caught it, or that you pinned it, or that it's in an equipment set or nothing caught it). Ctrl + right click an item for the slot menu: Add to category... pins it to a category you pick, Edit this category opens the Categories tab there, and the Auto Vendor and Protected marks clear each other.
- While a bag or bank window is open, its categories stay where they are (in their columns in Compact) and the window doesn't shrink, so selling or moving items doesn't shuffle them. Items inside a category still close up. Each time you open it, the window is arranged afresh, as short as its columns allow.
- `/sui` → Totem Bar is for Blizzard's shaman totem bar on WoW Forever, which SaftUI skins to match its action bars, each element's button bordered in that element's colour. Button size and Spacing set the size of its buttons and the gap between them, and the totem choices that open above a button follow. Above each element button, while that element has a totem down, a bar drains and a countdown shows how long the totem has left; both turn the Danger color (Global settings > Colors > Text) in the last 10 seconds. Show timer bar and Show timer text turn each off, Timer font sets the countdown's font (Small text to start with: one of SaftUI's fonts, or any typeface), Text position puts the time above the bar or on it, Text X and Y offset nudge it from there, and Timer bar height sets how tall the bar is. Hide totem bar hides the whole bar. On a shaman the bar is an edit mode frame: drag it in `/sui edit`, or set its Position at the end of the section. It starts where Blizzard had it.
- While a roll is open, each button on SaftUI's roll bars shows how many players have picked that choice so far (main and off spec needs both count as Need). A bar has Greed or Transmog, never both, so the one it has also shows the other choice's count at its top right, in that choice's colour. Hover the bar's icon or body for the item tooltip with everyone's choice, their roll once the game has it, who hasn't chosen yet, and who leads. It updates as people choose. Once you choose, the bar stays where it was, locked: your choice's button gets a blue border, the others dim, a small label names your choice, and nothing on it can be clicked. Its counts, tooltip and timer keep updating until the roll ends (the game awards the item, everyone passes, or its time runs out). Then the bar stays 3 seconds more with its timer stopped (counts that arrive in that time still show), then fades over half a second, so choosing last still shows your choice. A roll the game's loot history doesn't list (so it shows no counts) stays until its time runs out, even once the item is awarded. New rolls go after the bars already up, so a roll starting never moves one (a roll ending still closes its gap). The sample roll bars (`/sui` → Loot → Rolls → General → Test mode) show sample counts and choices, one of them locked as after you choose.
- `/sui` → Experience → General sets the experience bar's size and colours: Experience color for the bar, and Rested color for the bar while you have rested experience. The rested part past the bar shows in that colour at Rested bar alpha.
- The loot feed's two buttons, a funnel that opens the filter menu (which kinds of entry show, and Self Only) and an arrow that jumps back to the newest entry after you scroll the feed back, show while the feed has an entry on screen or the mouse is over it, and fade out with the last entry, so an empty feed leaves nothing on screen.
- The loot feed is kept per character across reloads and logouts: scroll the feed back to see earlier entries, hover one to see how long ago it happened. `/sui` → Loot → Feed → General → Clear history empties it.
- Every item your group rolls on gets one loot feed entry when its roll ends, naming the winner (or "All passed"); hover it to see everyone's choice and roll. When the winner loots the item, that goes into the same entry rather than a second one. Roll results show even with Self Only on. Untick Rolls in the feed's F menu to hide them; items you looted still show while Loot is ticked.
- Blizzard's loot window (the one that lists what a corpse or chest holds) is drawn in SaftUI's look by default under Thick and Thin: a panel, a card for each item and the item's quality color on its slot's border. `/sui` → Loot → Loot window has Enable, Theme, Background and Font; with Enable off, or under the Blizzard theme or None, the window stays as Blizzard draws it. Where it opens is Blizzard's setting.
- `/sui` → Skinning sets the Theme, Background and font of the Blizzard frames SaftUI skins: the buff icons, the swing timer bars (WoW Forever), the Cooldown Manager, Blizzard's color picker, and Hekili's and WeakAuras' icons. Blizzard's Cooldown Manager (Edit Mode's Essential and Utility cooldowns, tracked buffs and tracked bars) looks like SaftUI's action buttons: square icons in the theme's border, a flat cooldown swipe, out of range shown by the red tint alone, and SaftUI's pixel font on the countdown and the charges; a tracked bar is a flat bar like SaftUI's. Blizzard's Edit Mode still places, sizes and spaces it: SaftUI's border sits where Blizzard's round icon was, so neighbouring icons are Icon Padding apart (2px at Blizzard's default). Skinning → Cooldown Manager turns that skin off or on after a reload. Skinning → Color picker turns the color picker's skin off or on at once: a flat panel in the Theme, buttons and a hex box like SaftUI's (the button's edge turns blue under the mouse), and the title in the Window titles font; under the Blizzard theme it keeps Blizzard's look.
- Blizzard's objective tracker is skinned too: its headers lose Blizzard's banners and get a thin line under the title, quest titles and objectives use SaftUI's fonts (`/sui` → Blizzard's tracker → Font and Header font), progress and timer bars are flat bars like SaftUI's, quest item buttons look like SaftUI's action buttons, and the collapse buttons are small boxes with an arrow. It follows the Skinning page's Theme and Background; under the Blizzard theme, or with Skinning → Objective tracker off, it keeps Blizzard's look, and switching applies at once. Drag the tracker in `/sui edit` (its outline is the top of the tracker, which hangs down from there), or set its Position on `/sui` → Blizzard's tracker (while SaftUI's own tracker is on, Blizzard's is moved off screen and this position is unused, apart from the times Blizzard's tracker comes back for something SaftUI's doesn't draw yet, a dungeon or a tracked achievement, when it takes SaftUI's spot or sits beside it). It starts at Blizzard's default spot (or where you had moved it in Blizzard's Edit Mode), and its height stays Blizzard's: in Blizzard's default position it reaches down to the same line as before, otherwise it's the Height last set in Blizzard's Edit Mode, where the tracker can no longer be selected.
- SaftUI has an objective tracker of its own (the `SaftUI-Tracker` addon), on by default: `/sui` → Tracker → General → SaftUI's tracker turns it off after a reload, and another tracker addon makes it stand down (the switch is greyed, with the addon named). It lists your tracked quests (or, with Show on the Quests tab, the current zone's too) under their zone headers, the current zone first with a map pin, in a window sized by Width and Max height that scrolls past that (its scroll bar shows under the mouse) and moves in `/sui edit` (on a new profile it starts where you had moved Blizzard's tracker in Blizzard's Edit Mode, else at Blizzard's usual spot); Grow up hangs it from its bottom edge. Click shows the quest on the map or focuses it (Left click), Shift-click stops tracking it, right-click opens Blizzard's quest menu. A quest's item is a button of SaftUI's own that works in combat: In the row, in a column Beside the tracker, or Off, with an optional Nearest quest item button you can give a key (the Items tab, or Key Bindings → SaftUI). The Visibility tab fades or hides the tracker by combat, the mouse, instances, boss fights and Mythic+. It draws quests only for now: while a dungeon, scenario, achievement, recipe or another section has something to show, Blizzard's own tracker, kept off screen otherwise, shows in its place or beside it (the Blizzard's tracker tab). Its Completed and Failed objective colors are Global Settings → Colors → Objectives.
- Blizzard's armour durability figure (the little armoured man that shows when gear is damaged) can be moved: drag it in `/sui edit` (it shows there as Durability, even with nothing damaged, while Show hidden is on), or set its Position on `/sui` → Durability. Until you move it, it stays where Blizzard puts it; Move the durability figure off leaves it to Blizzard. Its tooltip also lists each damaged item with its durability percent, lowest first, and what repairing everything costs (Durability details in its tooltip).
- Your hearthstone's tooltip names the zone it's bound to (`Zone: Elwynn Forest`), not just the inn, and so do the hearthstone toys and Astral Recall. SaftUI learns the zone when you bind, or the next time you hearth home or step into the inn, and keeps it for each character; until then the line says it isn't known yet.
- If you use RestedXP Guides, SaftUI restyles its guide window in SaftUI's chat look: the chat panel and font, with fine divider lines. Its flight timer becomes a flat bar like SaftUI's cast bar. It follows SaftUI-Chat's panel and font and RestedXP's "Guide font size", and leaves RestedXP's newer (beta) guide window alone.
- If you use Spy Forever, `/sui` → Skinning → Spy restyles its Nearby window, alert popup, Statistics window and Kill on Sight button in SaftUI's look, in the Skinning page's Theme, Background and font (and the window titles in the Window titles font). Spy keeps all its behavior, including clicking a row to target. While the skin is on, Spy's own Border and window colors aren't used, but each alert keeps its kind's border color. The Kill on Sight button moves onto SaftUI's target frame, since SaftUI's unit frames replace the one Spy used. The Statistics window's scroll bar and drop-down stay Spy's and Blizzard's. Turning the skin off or on needs a reload; under the Blizzard theme Spy keeps its own look.
- When you enter a zone, its name shows with a line under it giving the zone's level range and who holds it, such as "Level 10–20 · Alliance" ("Level 70" when it's one level). Alliance shows in blue, Horde in red and Neutral (a contested zone or a sanctuary) in yellow by default: the Factions colors of `/sui` → Global Settings → Colors. Who holds it is the game's PvP status where you stand, and the line takes the place of the game's own "(Contested Territory)" line. Without a level range only the faction shows; in a dungeon, raid or other instance there's no faction, and the difficulty shows under the area name instead.
- On the world map, hovering a zone adds a line under its name and level range saying who holds it, in the same colors. For the zone you're in it follows the game's PvP status, when the game gives one; otherwise, and for the other zones, it comes from a list of each client's zones SaftUI ships. A map that isn't a zone on the list (a continent, a dungeon, a cave) gets no line.
- Profiles live under `/sui` → Profiles, and switching applies live. The unit frames have profiles of their own inside it (one look for every unit frame, to switch by role or raid size): on each unit frame's page (the nameplate pages too), the Unit dropdown's line shows Profile, a dropdown that switches it, and Manage, which opens a small window to make, copy, rename, delete, export and import them (a search for "unit frame profile" finds them). Edit mode's own controls are on its bar, not in the footer.
- `/rl` reloads the UI.

## Development

- Plain Lua and XML, with no build step. Edit, then `/reload` in game.
- `tests/run` runs the Lua tests in `tests/`. They check SaftUI's logic on Lua 5.1 against stubbed WoW API, and the first run sets up `tests/.venv` with Python's lupa. They're for agents and development: changes are still checked in game, where BugSack is recommended for reading errors.
- Blizzard's UI source for reference: [Gethe/wow-ui-source](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source), on the `forever` and `live` branches.
- Agent and contributor conventions are in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) and `.agents/`. Design specs, plans and the followups queue are in `docs/`.
